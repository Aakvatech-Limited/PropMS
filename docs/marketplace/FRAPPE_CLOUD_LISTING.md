## Long description (no installation commands)

### Property Management Solution for ERPNext

PropMS brings property leasing, facilities operations, and supporting processes into ERPNext. Property teams can manage properties and units, lease agreements, lease invoice schedules, utility meter readings, maintenance material billing, daily checklists, key and tool custody, and security or outsourcing attendance.

**Property and lease management:** Maintain property records, ownership information, leases, lease items, tenancy terms, renewals, and invoicing schedules.

**Rent and billing:** Work with ERPNext customers, items, Sales Invoices, accounts and cost centers. Review rent and utility invoice reports.

**Facilities and utility operations:** Record meter readings, maintenance usage and operational checklists, and track asset custody and attendance for property operations.

**Integration:** Built for ERPNext and the Frappe Framework, with links to native selling, stock, accounting, and reporting workflows. Requires ERPNext and relevant app dependencies.

**Publisher:** Aakvatech Limited, Tanzania.
